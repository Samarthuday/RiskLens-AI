Demo disclaimer: the stream and stored activity are generated synthetic data. Historical SAML-D metrics are offline research results from a synthetic dataset. No real payment actions occur. Probabilities are produced only by the validated model artifact; absent that artifact, the UI reports score unavailability. All recommendations require human review.
